# IACEC website — guide for staff

This guide explains how to update the website. You do not need any technical knowledge.

## Signing in

1. Go to **iacecglobal.com/admin**.
2. Enter your email address and password, then press **Sign in**.
3. To change your password, open **My account** in the menu on the left.

If you forget your password, ask an administrator to reset it (Users → Reset password).

## What you can do depends on your role

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Administrator** | Everything, including users, site settings, forms and all submissions |
| **Editor** | Pages, collections (programmes, news…) and the media library |
| **Sponsorship Coordinator** | Read and manage sponsorship submissions only (including confidential child applications) |

## Saving and publishing

- Nothing changes on the website until you press **Save** (bottom of the screen).
- After saving, the website updates within a few seconds. Open the page (or press **View page**) to check.
- If you try to leave with unsaved changes, you will be warned.

---

## Editing a page

1. Open **Pages** and choose a page (for example *Home* or *About Us*).
2. The page is made of **sections**, in the order they appear on the website. Click a section to open it.
3. Change any text, button or picture, then press **Save changes**.

Useful tools on each section:

- **Drag the dotted handle** (⋮⋮) to move a section up or down.
- **Eye icon** — hide a section without deleting it (press again to show it).
- **Copy icon** — duplicate a section (handy for adding another call-to-action band).
- **Bin icon** — remove a section.
- **Add a section** (bottom of the list) — choose a new block, such as a call-to-action band or a rich-text block.

Each button has two boxes: the **text** shown on the button and the **link** (for example `/donate` or `https://…`). Links to another part of the same page use `#`, for example `/community-support#sponsor`.

On the right you can change the page's title, its **web address**, whether it is **published**, and how it looks on Google and when shared (SEO title, description, sharing image).

**Templates:** *Programme page template* and *News article template* control the parts that every programme page and every news article share (the "At a glance" box labels, the "More from IACEC" heading, the closing band).

### Creating a new page

**Pages → New page**, give it a title and web address. It starts as a draft with a heading section. Add sections, then switch on **Published** and save. Remember to add the page to a menu (Site settings → Header & navigation, or Footer).

## Collections (lists used across the site)

Open **Collections** to manage:

- **Programmes** — the programme cards and each programme's own page. *Feature on the home page* chooses the three cards on the home page.
- **News articles** — write stories with the text editor. Tick *Featured* to show an article first in the newsroom; *Show the date* displays the publication date.
- **Events** and **Past conferences** — appear under News & Events → Events.
- **Resources** — downloadable files (upload the PDF in the media library first).
- **Team members** — appear on the About page once at least one is published.
- **Pillars, Values, Milestones, Sponsorship steps, Donation funds, Volunteer areas, Partnership types, Partners, Testimonies.**

In each list you can search, **drag items to change their order** on the website, and switch items on or off (Published / Approved / Public) without deleting them. Click an item to edit it; press **Add …** to create a new one.

## Pictures and documents (Media library)

- **Upload files** or drag photos onto the page. Photos are resized and optimised automatically so the site stays fast on mobile data.
- Click a photo to:
  - write a **description** (alt text) — what the photo shows, for people using screen readers;
  - set its **focal point** by clicking the most important part of the photo, so it stays visible when the photo is cropped;
  - **Replace file** — the new photo replaces the old one everywhere on the website;
  - **Delete** it.
- Wherever a picture appears in a page or collection, press **Choose** / **Change** to pick from the library or upload a new one.

**Child protection:** only publish a photo or full name of a child when consent has been recorded.

## Submissions (forms filled in on the website)

Open **Submissions** to see messages, training requests, invitations, sponsorship interest, volunteer and partnership enquiries, giving details and child sponsorship applications.

- Filter by form and status, or search.
- Open a submission to read it, change its **status** (New → In progress → Done → Archived) and add **internal notes** (never shown to the public).
- **Export CSV (Excel)** — choose a form first, then export; the file opens in Excel.
- Child sponsorship applications (marked with a padlock) are confidential. They are visible only to administrators and sponsorship coordinators, and are not sent by email. Do not copy, forward or publish them.

## Forms

**Forms** (administrators) lets you change each form's questions, labels, drop-down options, which questions are required, the button text, the thank-you message, the automatic reply, and **who receives the notification email**. Leave the recipients empty to use the default notification email in Site settings.

## Newsletter

**Newsletter** lists everyone who joined the mailing list (footer, contact page). You can export the list to Excel or remove someone who asks to be removed.

## Site settings

**Site settings** (administrators) has tabs for:

- **Identity** — logo, name, favicon;
- **Contact details** — email, phone numbers, city, map link, WhatsApp number (used by the green "Chat with us" button);
- **Social links** — icons appear in the footer when filled in;
- **Header & navigation** — the menu, the "Get Involved" drop-down, the mobile menu, the Contact and Donate buttons;
- **Footer** — description, link columns, newsletter text, bottom links;
- **Giving methods** — bank transfer, Mobile Money and international details. While these are empty, the Donate page shows "Giving details are being finalised.";
- **SEO & sharing** — default title, description and sharing image;
- **Announcement banner** — an optional strip at the top of every page;
- **Notifications** — the default email address for form notifications.

## Users

**Users** (administrators): **Invite someone** creates an account and shows a temporary password once (it is also emailed if email is set up). Change someone's role, switch an account to inactive when a person leaves, or reset a password. There must always be at least one active administrator.

## Need help?

If something looks wrong on the website after a change, open the page or item again — the last saved version is what the website shows. Undo your change and save again, or contact the person who maintains the website.
